Capital Will Accelerate Nationwide Clinic Growth And Expand Zarminali’s Proprietary Technology Platform CHICAGO, JAN 21, 2026 - Zarminali Pediatrics , the first outpatient pediatric destination purpose-built to provide integrated primary and specialty care nationwide, today announced a $110M Series A funding round. Healthier Capital led the round, along with participation from existing investor General Catalyst , and new investor K2 HealthVentures .
