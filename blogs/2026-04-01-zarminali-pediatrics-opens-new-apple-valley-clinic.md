Zarminali Pediatrics, the company building the first outpatient pediatric destination purpose-built to provide elevated, seamlessly coordinated primary care nationwide, is announcing the official opening of its newest clinic in Apple Valley. This is Zarminali's third clinic in the state, alongside locations in Sartell and Eden Prairie. Having opened on March 31, this brand-new facility expands access to personalized, coordinated care for Minnesota families.
