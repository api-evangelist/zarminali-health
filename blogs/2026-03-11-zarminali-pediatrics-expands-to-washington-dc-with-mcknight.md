Zarminali Pediatrics, the company building the first outpatient pediatric destination purpose-built to provide elevated, seamlessly coordinated primary care nationwide, is expanding its presence in Washington, DC. The long-standing McKnight Pediatrics became part of Zarminali Pediatrics on March 10. McKnight Pediatrics, which has been trusted by Washington, DC-area families for generations, has now joined the Zarminali Pediatrics family.
