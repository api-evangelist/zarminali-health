Zarminali Pediatrics, the company building the first outpatient pediatric destination purpose-built to provide elevated, seamlessly coordinated primary care nationwide, is expanding into Maryland as the long-standing South River Pediatrics clinics in Dunkirk and Edgewater join the Zarminali network. South River Pediatrics, which has been trusted by Maryland families for decades, will now operate under the Zarminali Pediatrics name. This marks the company's first locations in the state and will provide families in the area with access to a new standard of coordinated pediatric care.
