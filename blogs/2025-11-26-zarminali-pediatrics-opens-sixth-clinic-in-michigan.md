Zarminali Pediatrics, building the first outpatient pediatric destination purpose-built to provide elevated, seamlessly coordinated primary care nationwide, will officially unveil its newest clinic in West Bloomfield, Michigan. The clinic is Zarminali’s sixth location in the state and will open on December 4. Built from the ground up, the brand new facility is designed with the needs of modern families in mind and is now accepting new patients.
